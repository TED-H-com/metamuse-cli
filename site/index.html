活動後的留言想整理？影片裡的一段話想引用？讓你慣用的 AI 助理幫忙判讀與撰寫，MetaMuse 負責保存原文、搜尋段落，把草稿與引用一起留下。

A familiar task你可能也遇過

The event is over. The feedback is still scattered.

活動結束了，留言與影片還散在各處。

You want to know what people asked for, find a useful quote, and write a recap you can stand behind. Give your assistant permitted comments or local subtitles. MetaMuse keeps those sources available so you can check a draft against the original words.

想知道大家提了哪些需求、找出一段值得引用的話，再寫成一份能核對的回顧。把你有權使用的留言或本地字幕交給助理整理，MetaMuse 留下來源，讓你隨時回頭確認原文。

How it works

運作方式

From your files to a sourced draft

從你的資料，走到有來源的草稿

Bring a comment export or local subtitles. Find the passages you need, write with your assistant, then keep the draft and its sources together.

帶入留言匯出檔或本地字幕，找出需要的原文，和 AI 助理一起寫，再把草稿與引用一起留下。

When it helps適合拿來做什麼

Less hunting for the original. More time for the recap.

少一點翻找原文，多一點時間整理重點。

Start with local files. Add an assistant when you want help interpreting and writing.

先從本地檔案開始，需要判讀與撰寫時，再交給你的 AI 助理協助。

01

Review event feedback

整理活動回饋

Find comments about the next event, accessibility requests or missing materials. Your assistant can turn the selected evidence into a recap.

找出對下一場活動的建議、無障礙需求或索取講義的留言，再讓助理依選好的原文寫回顧。

02

Find a transcript passage

找回字幕段落

Import local SRT or VTT files and search for the words you want to quote.

匯入本地 SRT 或 VTT 字幕，用關鍵字找出想引用的段落。

03

Check where a claim came from

核對一句話的來源

Keep source references and exact text revisions alongside a draft, so you can compare the wording with the original.

將來源引用與原文版本連同草稿保存，需要時可以對照文字。

04

Use your usual AI assistant

接上慣用的 AI 助理

Connect through CLI or MCP. The assistant asks questions and writes; MetaMuse handles the local records.

透過 CLI 或 MCP 連接。助理負責提問與撰寫，MetaMuse 負責保存本地資料。

05

Ask Muse a follow-up

選擇向 Muse 追問

Muse is optional and needs separate setup and human approval. Real-account use is still awaiting LIVE verification.

Muse 是可選的協作者，需要另外設定及人類授權。真實帳號使用仍待 LIVE 驗證。

06

Keep a file you can use

留下能接著使用的文件

Export Markdown, text, CSV, JSONL or a bundle for your own review and editing. Facebook Page text publishing is experimental and requires separate setup and your exact approval.

匯出 Markdown、文字、CSV、JSONL 或資料套件，方便後續閱讀與編輯。Facebook 粉專文字發文仍在實驗階段，需要另外設定，並由你核對批准。

Read it first. Then approve the post.

先看全文與粉專，再決定要不要發

If you want to publish, MetaMuse prepares the exact text and destination for your review. You approve that post before anything is sent.

想把草稿發到 Facebook 粉專時，先看這次要送出的全文與目的地。由你核對並批准，才會送出。

搜尋、草稿、匯出，實際畫面在這裡。

Before you start開始之前

A few practical questions.

幾個你可能想問的問題。

The first example uses local synthetic files, so you can see the result before connecting accounts or an assistant.

第一個範例只用本地合成檔案，先看到結果，再決定是否連接帳號或 AI 助理。

01

Do I need a Muse account?

一定要有 Muse 帳號嗎？

No account is needed for local import, search, drafts or exports. Muse is a separately configured, optional discussion partner.

本地匯入、搜尋、草稿與匯出不需要帳號。Muse 是需要另外設定的選用討論對象。

02

Will the example write with AI?

範例會直接用 AI 寫回顧嗎？

It saves a clearly labelled prewritten recap. For your own work, connect an AI assistant or provide the text yourself.

範例保存的是清楚標記的預寫回顧。處理自己的資料時，可以連接 AI 助理，或自行提供文字。

03

Can I paste a Facebook or video URL?

可以直接貼 Facebook 或影片網址嗎？

Remote collectors need their own setup and operator approval. They currently have synthetic tests, with real-account LIVE validation pending. Start with local files.

遠端收集需要各自的設定與操作者授權。目前已有合成資料測試，真實帳號 LIVE 驗證仍待完成。建議先從本地檔案開始。

04

Is this a web app?

這是網頁版工具嗎？

This page introduces a tool that runs on your computer through a terminal or a connected AI assistant. The source preview can be installed today; a packaged release is not published yet.

這裡是介紹頁。工具在你的電腦上，透過終端機或已連接的 AI 助理操作。目前可從原始碼安裝預覽版，尚未發布套件。

打開你的第一份有來源回顧。

Install Git and uv first, then install the source preview as a command. The alpha is being prepared and is not on PyPI yet. Installation downloads Python and dependencies; the demo needs no account or AI service.

先安裝 Git 與 uv，再把原始碼預覽版裝成指令。alpha 套件正在準備，尚未上架 PyPI。安裝會下載 Python 與依賴；範例不需要帳號或 AI 服務。

What you can do today現在能拿來做什麼

Find the original. Keep a draft you can check.

找到原文，留下能核對的草稿。

Start with files on your computer. Search, save drafts and export without a Muse account. Muse and remote collection are not yet verified with real accounts.

先從你電腦上的檔案開始。不需要 Muse 帳號，也能搜尋資料、保存草稿、匯出文件。Muse 與遠端收集尚未以真實帳號驗證。

Start with your own files

用自己的檔案就能開始

  • Import saved comments and subtitles
    Bring together the saved comments, articles and subtitle files you already have, so you can find them later.
  • 整理手邊的留言與字幕
    把已匯出的留言、文章和字幕放進同一個專案，之後方便查找。
  • Find a comment or transcript passage
    Search for a word such as subtitles, then read the matching text and its source.
  • 找出想引用的留言或字幕段落
    例如搜尋「字幕」，找到相關留言，再打開原文與來源核對。
  • Keep a draft with its sources
    Save text you or your assistant wrote, together with the original material you cited.
  • 保存附來源的草稿
    把你或 AI 助理寫好的文字連同引用保存，之後仍能對照原文。
  • Export a file you can edit
    Keep your draft and material as text documents or spreadsheet-friendly files for further editing.
  • 匯出能繼續編輯的文件
    把草稿與資料匯出成文字文件或試算表可讀的檔案，留在電腦上繼續編輯。
  • Work with your AI assistant
    Connect your assistant to MetaMuse, then ask it to find material and draft a recap with citations.
  • 讓慣用的 AI 助理幫忙整理
    先接上你慣用的 AI 助理，再請它搜尋資料、整理需求，寫一份附來源的回顧。

Connect an account to use these workflows

接上帳號，就能使用的流程

  • Ask Muse follow-up questions
    Choose source material, ask a question, continue the discussion and cite the replies in your draft. Use connect muse to bind your account. Real-account acceptance is pending. Setup and steps.
  • 把選好的資料拿去問 Muse
    選好資料問問題、繼續追問，再把回答與來源一起存進草稿。現在有連線指令，不用手動編輯設定檔。真實帳號驗收仍待完成。連線與操作步驟。
  • Collect a Facebook post or remote video
    Collect one specified post and its comments, or video metadata and available subtitles. Review the exact target and approve the read first. Real-account acceptance is pending. Setup and steps.
  • 收集 Facebook 貼文或遠端影片
    指定一篇貼文收集原文與留言，或取得影片資訊與可用字幕。先看網址與讀取範圍，批准後再執行。真實帳號驗收仍待完成。連線與操作步驟。
  • Publish a text post to a Facebook Page
    Connect your Page, review the full draft and destination, then approve one send and keep its receipt. Real-account acceptance is pending. Setup and steps.
  • 把文字草稿發到 Facebook 粉專
    接上你管理的粉專，核對草稿全文與目的地，批准後送出，並保存與核對貼文回執。真實帳號驗收仍待完成。連線與操作步驟。
